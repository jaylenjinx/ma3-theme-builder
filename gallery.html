Share your theme

Anyone can add a theme. Submissions go through a short form on GitHub and appear here once they're approved.

  1. Build itStart from any theme in the editor and change the colours until it looks right on the live screen.
  2. Press Share to galleryIt's under Export. GitHub opens with your theme already filled in as a short code. Add a name and a line about it, then submit. You need a free GitHub account.
  3. Wait for approvalEach theme is checked before it goes live. Once it's approved it shows up under Community within a few minutes.
